Partner FAQ

Quick answers before the call.

Is the portal really free for our firm?

Yes — permanently, with no client minimum. The portal is monetised through the merchant subscription (AED 75/mo per merchant). Your firm pays nothing, and we pay you 20% of every merchant you bring on.

What happens if a client we refer cancels?

The payout simply stops on the next billing cycle. There's no clawback, no minimum commitment period, no penalties.

Do we have to push Heifer on every client?

No. There's no exclusivity and no quota. Most partner firms recommend Heifer only to the cash-heavy clients where Xero/Zoho doesn't fit — bazaar shops, kiosks, small grocers, market vendors. That's where the data is broken today.

Can the portal be co-branded with our firm?

Yes — firm logo and colours on the merchant-facing reports, and a "Managed by [Firm Name]" line in the bot's quarterly VAT summary. Available from your second active client onwards.

Is Heifer an FTA-registered tax agent?

No — we're the software layer. You remain the tax agent of record for your clients. We produce FTA-spec invoices and quarterly returns; you review, sign, and file.

How quickly can a new client be live?

Typically 5–7 business days from the merchant onboarding call. We migrate their existing books, set up the Telegram bot, and run a 30-min walkthrough — entirely on our side.
